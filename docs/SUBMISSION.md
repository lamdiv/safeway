# SafeWay — submission draft

**Tagline:** When access changes, guidance changes.

SafeWay is an operator-supervised evacuation exercise that treats directions as expiring, evidence-backed decisions. A closed approach disappears immediately. A replacement remains withheld until preapproved geometry is clear of staged hazard, property approval is current, exterior evidence matches, an entrance is explicitly open and the indoor check-in connection is verified.
